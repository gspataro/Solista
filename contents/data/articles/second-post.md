## 📚 Building momentum

Creating content consistently is what turns a static site into something meaningful.

With Solista, adding a new post is as easy as creating a new file:

- no database
- no complex setup
- just write and build

## 🔄 Content relationships

By now, you’ve probably noticed something important: content is not isolated.

For example, this post can link back to the first one:

👉 Go back to [Hello World](orchestra://articles/hello-world.md)

But we can also link a page

👉 Go back [Home](orchestra://index)

Or even an archive:

👉 Go back to the [Blog](orchestra://blog)

And thanks to Solista, these links will always resolve correctly, even if your URLs change.

## 🧠 Thinking in structure

As your site grows, structure becomes essential.

You can start organizing content by:

- categories
- future tags (if you decide to add them 😉)
- custom fields

This gives you the flexibility to build:

- blogs
- documentation
- portfolios
- or anything else

## ⚙️ Under the hood

Even if it feels simple, there’s a lot happening behind the scenes:

- content gets parsed
- schemas resolve relationships
- pages are generated
- everything is cached for performance

And yet, from your perspective, it’s just writing Markdown.

## 🚧 What’s next?

At this point, you can:

- create more posts
- experiment with categories
- customize your theme
- explore how archives are generated

Or just enjoy the process of building your site, one article at a time.
